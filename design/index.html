What we do

From understanding problems
to building products.

  1. 01

    Research

    Understand what needs to change. We explore usability and learning motivation to guide design. For Genome Cache, that meant identifying readability and consistency issues, then shaping a more engaging, game-based learning experience.

    Genome Cache
  2. 02

    UI Design

    Clear interfaces, consistent systems. We make complex information easier to understand and build the visual foundations that keep products coherent. FLIR METERLiNK brings live readings, alerts and measurement trends into one mobile experience.

    FLIR METERLiNK App ↗
  3. 03

    In-house

    Our ideas, made tangible. We explore everyday needs through product design and prototyping. Weekday brings floor-plan seat selection, desk reservations and workspace management together for a simpler day at the office.

    Weekday
  4. 04

    AI built for everyday work. We connect clear interactions with company knowledge and practical controls. Office Power brings AI agents into work channels, with management tools for setup, budgets, keywords and permissions.

How we work

Five steps, no surprises

Every project moves through the same rhythm. You see progress every week, not a big reveal in week twelve.

1

Discover

Understand the business, the users and the constraints.

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • Business analysis
  • Personas
2

Define

Agree on the problem before we solve it.

  • User flows
  • Information architecture
  • Scope & goals
3

Design

Sketch fast, then refine what works.

  • Wireframes
  • Visual design
  • Interactive prototypes
4

Validate

Put it in front of real people and listen.

  • Usability testing
  • Design review with dev
  • Iteration
5

Deliver

Ship it together, then keep improving.

  • Dev-ready specs
  • Design system updates
  • Design QA
🤝

Designers in the room

We join the daily stand-up with engineers and PMs. Questions get answered in minutes, not tickets.

🧪

Test early, test often

A rough prototype in front of users beats a polished guess. We validate before we polish.

🧩

Build once, reuse everywhere

Every component we make goes into the system, so the next product starts further ahead.
