How we work

From insight to proof, faster

Insight

Find the signal before choosing a direction.

  • Business & user context
  • Research synthesis
  • Constraints & opportunities

Figure out

Explore more directions, then focus on the one that matters.

  • AI-assisted ideation
  • User flows & structure
  • Scope & priorities

Prototype

Turn the strongest direction into something people can use.

  • Interface variations
  • Working prototypes
  • AI-assisted coding

Verify

Test the experience against real users and real constraints.

  • Usability testing
  • Design & engineering review
  • Refine & ship
